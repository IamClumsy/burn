# Burned: Miami Idle: ideas for later

What's built is listed at the bottom. This is what's left, roughly in the order I'd do it.

## 1. Before calling it finished

- **Real-device playtest:** phone and tablet widths, a long session, and a couple of Reinstates. Watch Vercel's INP numbers after each deploy (the flashback look was reworked once already to keep clicks fast).
- **Pacing pass after a long playthrough:** several Reinstates in, check boss timing, favor prices, how fast the late Seasons open, and how long the last foes on The List take (Burke $110B, Sonya $180B, Kendrick $300B).
- **Accessibility pass:** keyboard focus for pop-ups and the case tile, a reduced-motion setting, and contrast on the dimmed cards.
- **Share image:** a 1200×630 preview for links, using the header art and the photos.

## 2. An ending that lands

- **A bigger finish:** crossing off the last name already gives an epilogue pop-up with the crew, saved in the Case File, plus +15 favors and +25% income. A "keep playing" screen with a recap of your run (time, cases, foes, answers in Sam's story) would give it more weight.
- **Late-story beats:** Nate's Season 6 fate could get the same quiet "after the story beat" treatment that Carla has.

## 3. More to do mid-game

- **More flashbacks:** The Fall of Sam Axe set the pattern (a case with an Admiral's inquiry, choices, a showdown, its own look and a replay page in the menu). Other one-off stories could reuse it.
- **Photos on mission cards:** show the client and the villain next to each episode, using the portraits already in the game.
- **More random events:** Nate's excuses, Madeline's phone tree, a Sam story, a Barry rate-card moment. Each is a few lines of text.
- **More cast:** Beatriz and Amanda as characters, Diego Garza and Max as flavor, more one-episode villains as Rogues.

## 4. Smaller ideas

- **Browser notifications:** an opt-in "a boss is at the door" alert when the tab is in the background (the tab title already flags it).
- **Cloud save or a short save code:** export works, but a long string is awkward on a phone.
- **Sound pass:** a few more cues and a volume slider rather than on/off.
- **Install as an app:** works offline, with a home-screen icon.
- **Seasonal flavor:** a one-line recap card when each new Season opens.
- **Tablet check:** the in-between widths haven't had a proper look.

## Built so far, for reference

- **The core loop:** operations, upgrades, covers, gadgets (including the rare Submarine), a crew of allies with abilities and a bench of fixers, Heat and the Organization's attention (with an info view and net bars), Lay Low, Reinstate with a preview, Credibility, favors, perks, medals, and offline progress with a Welcome back card.
- **The show:** 111 episodes as missions (the board favors unseen episodes and the current Season), The List of 18 foes plus ghosts from the past, Rogues, Open Cases, the Case File and Spy notebook, Michael's File, handlers' errands, the epilogue, and a Narrator history.
- **Automation:** auto-take clients and auto-send crew, unlocked by Credibility, costing favors to buy and a cut of each job, with off switches.
- **Crew rules:** Nate wanders off and comes back changed, Madeline joins in Season 3 and only helps with kids and older neighbors, Jesse is a "?????" until his episode, and a fourth mission slot takes only cases an ally is helping with.
- **The Fall of Sam Axe:** offered after Beatriz turns up in Depth Perception (or once Season 6 opens), a seven-step flashback case told at a Navy inquiry. Every step opens with the Admiral's questions, there's a Veracruz showdown, a bowling-alley nod to Michael's cameo, and everything else pauses while it runs. It has its own aged look, rewards (a medal, the Chuck Finley cover, a sharper Sam), a page in the menu and a replay.
- **Economy:** operation prices rise 15% each for the first 50, then 6%. Foes unlock on full earnings (every fee, including what Michael hands back), and Seasons, covers and story on his 10% take. Michael's File shows both with the wait to each next unlock.
- **Quality:** 369 tests (data integrity, math, a headless game loop), number safety and sanitized saves, and photo portraits with drawn sketches as a fallback.
