# Burned: Miami Idle: ideas for later

What's built is listed at the bottom. This is what's left, roughly in the order I'd do it.

## 1. Before calling it finished

- **Real-device playtest:** phone and tablet widths, a long session, and a couple of Reinstates. Watch Vercel's INP numbers after each deploy (the flashback look was reworked once already to keep clicks fast).
- **Pacing pass after a long playthrough:** several Reinstates in, check boss timing, favor prices, how fast the late Seasons open, and how long the last foes on The List take (Season 7 opens at $40B; Burke $44B, Sonya $72B, Kendrick $120B).
- **Accessibility pass:** keyboard focus rings and reduced-motion are in; still to do is a check of contrast on the dimmed cards and focus handling inside pop-ups.

## 2. An ending that lands

- **A bigger finish:** built as "Your Run": a recap of the run in the menu, which opens when the burn is lifted. Nate's and Madeline's scenes (after "Shock Wave" and "Reckoning") close out their stories.

## 3. More to do mid-game

- **More flashbacks:** The Fall of Sam Axe set the pattern (a case with an Admiral's inquiry, choices, a showdown, its own look and a replay page in the menu), and Nigeria and First Contact reuse it as data. Other one-off stories can do the same.
- **Photos on mission cards:** show the client and the villain next to each episode, using the portraits already in the game.
- **More random events:** Nate's excuses, Madeline's phone tree, a Sam story, a Barry rate-card moment. Each is a few lines of text.
- **More cast:** Beatriz and Amanda as characters, Diego Garza and Max as flavor, more one-episode villains as Rogues.

## 4. Smaller ideas

- **Browser notifications:** an opt-in "a boss is at the door" alert when the tab is in the background (the tab title already flags it).
- **Cloud save or a short save code:** export works, but a long string is awkward on a phone.
- **Sound pass:** a few more cues and a volume slider rather than on/off.
- **Seasonal flavor:** a one-line recap card when each new Season opens.
- **Tablet check:** phone and tablet widths have had a look; a real device would still be worth it.

## Built so far, for reference

- **The core loop:** operations, upgrades, covers, gadgets (including the rare Submarine), a crew of allies with abilities and a bench of fixers, Heat and the Organization's attention (with an info view and net bars), Lay Low, Reinstate with a preview, Credibility, favors, perks, medals (and a level-20 cap on the endless referral upgrade), and offline progress with a Welcome back card.
- **The show:** 111 episodes as missions (the board favors unseen episodes and the current Season), The List of 18 foes plus ghosts from the past, Rogues, Open Cases, the Case File and Spy notebook, Michael's File, handlers' errands, the epilogue, and a Narrator history.
- **Automation:** auto-take clients and auto-send crew, unlocked by Credibility, costing favors to buy and a cut of each job, with off switches.
- **Crew rules:** Nate wanders off and comes back changed, Madeline is in the Crew from the start but only helps on cases from Season 3, Episode 2 on (kids and older neighbors), Jesse is a "?????" until his episode, and a fourth mission slot takes only cases an ally is helping with.
- **The Fall of Sam Axe:** offered after Beatriz turns up in Depth Perception (or once Season 6 opens), a seven-step flashback case told at a Navy inquiry. Every step opens with the Admiral's questions, there's a Veracruz showdown, a bowling-alley nod to Michael's cameo, and everything else pauses while it runs. It has its own aged look, rewards (a medal, the Chuck Finley cover, a sharper Sam), a page in the menu and a replay.
- **Nigeria:** told to Sam at a bar once the Pilot case is done (or Season 2 opens): five steps in three acts, Sam's running commentary, and a chase to the airport with Boris's men behind you. A medal and a replay page.
- **First Contact:** how Michael met Fiona, told as soon as Season 6 opens (with a narration card: Fiona in prison after turning herself in), until her release in 606 brings her back. Five steps in three acts, each question offering Michael's fuzzy version or Fiona's, a prison call to steer the start, no showdown, a medal and a replay page.
- **Nate and Madeline:** Nate's last scene after "Shock Wave", Madeline's after "Reckoning", each leaving a small permanent bonus and a medal.
- **Economy:** operation prices rise 15% each for the first 50, then 6%. Foes unlock on full earnings (every fee, including what Michael hands back), and Seasons, covers and story on what he keeps. Michael's File shows both with the wait to each next unlock.
- **An app:** installable (Add to Home Screen), plays offline, shows its version in Settings, notices a newer build when you return to it and reloads after saving, and uses bottom sheets and gentle motion on phones (and none for anyone who asks for less).
- **After the finale:** Michael's File reads "Burn lifted" with a cabin address, and heat, the Organization's attention, handlers and boss encounters stop.
- **Quality:** 417 tests (data integrity, math, a headless game loop), a wiki check of every case's client and villain, number safety and sanitized saves, and photo portraits with drawn sketches as a fallback.
